Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026

InfraSim ("InfraSim," "we," "us") is a mobile system-design game. This policy explains what data the app and its backend collect, why, and how you can control it. InfraSim is fully playable offline as a guest — you never need to create an account to play the tutorial, campaign, Sandbox, Survival, or use the Learn encyclopedia.

1. Data we collect

1.1 If you play as a guest (no account)

Your progress — campaign stars, saved architectures, settings, and run history — is stored only on your device in a local database. We do not receive it, and it is not backed up to our servers unless you create an account.

1.2 If you create an account

Creating an account is optional and is used only to sync progress across devices, keep a Daily Challenge leaderboard, and restore purchases. Accounts are provided through Supabase Auth and, on iOS, Sign in with Apple, or a Google sign-in. We collect:

  • Email address (or the private relay address Apple provides if you choose "Hide My Email").
  • A display name or handle you choose, shown next to your score on public Daily Challenge leaderboards. Do not use your real name if you would rather stay pseudonymous.
  • Gameplay progress synced from your device: stars, scores, completed levels, saved architectures, and achievement state.

1.3 Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are purchased through Apple's App Store or Google Play and processed by RevenueCat, our subscription management provider. We receive a purchase record (product purchased, active/expired status, renewal date, platform) so the app can unlock Pro features — we never receive your card number, billing address, or other payment details. Apple and Google handle payment directly.

1.4 Diagnostics and analytics

ServiceWhat it collectsWhy
Sentry (crash/error reporting) Stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, and a device-generated installation identifier. No architecture contents or free text you typed. Find and fix crashes and bugs.
PostHog (product analytics) Anonymous or pseudonymous event names (e.g. "level completed", "paywall viewed"), coarse device/OS info, and a random installation identifier. Personally identifying fields are scrubbed before sending. We do not send architecture contents or free text as event data. Understand which features are used and where players get stuck, so we can improve the game.
Push notifications (expo-notifications) A device push token, only if you enable notifications. Daily Challenge and streak reminders you opt into. You can disable this in Settings or your device's notification settings at any time.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location — the app does not request those permissions.
  • We do not share your data with third parties except the service providers named above (Supabase, RevenueCat, Sentry, PostHog, Apple, Google), each acting only to provide the function described.

3. How your data is stored and protected

Guest data lives only in your device's local storage. Account data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase with row-level security, so your account can only read and write its own rows — nobody else's progress, saved architectures, or run history. Leaderboard entries expose only your chosen display name and score, never your email.

4. Your choices

  • Play entirely offline as a guest and share nothing with us.
  • Disable notifications at any time in Settings.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data — see our account deletion page for the exact steps.
  • Contact support@infrasim.app with any privacy question or request.

5. Children's privacy

InfraSim is rated for a general audience (Apple 4+ / Google Everyone) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. International data transfers

Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries where they operate infrastructure. By using an account, you consent to this transfer as necessary to provide the service.
