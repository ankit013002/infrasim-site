About

InfraSim is a mobile system-design game. Design a cloud architecture on a canvas (DNS, CDN, load balancer, compute, cache, database, queue) deploy it, and watch simulated traffic flow through the graph. Metrics move, bottlenecks appear, costs accrue, failures propagate, and incidents force a reaction. The simulation runs entirely on your device and provisions nothing real.
